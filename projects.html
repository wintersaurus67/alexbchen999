Research · solo

A Sharp Piecewise Generalization of Nesbitt's Inequality

preprint · 2026 α ∈ (0,1] · phase at log₂(3/2)

Establishes a sharp lower bound for the cyclic power sum ∑(a/(b+c))α on positive reals for α ∈ (0,1], with a phase transition at α* = log₂(3/2). Above the threshold, the symmetric minimum is sharp; below it, the infimum is 2, approached as one variable vanishes. Recovers classical Nesbitt at α=1.

Random Schrödinger Operators & Anderson Localization

w/ Mei, Shaw, Tang advised by Prof. Wencai Liu

Studying eigenvector decay in discrete random Schrödinger operators on the lattice, with i.i.d. Bernoulli potentials on the diagonal of a tridiagonal matrix. Building computational frameworks to numerically observe exponential localization — distinguishing real decay from rounding artifact — and connecting numerics to the analytic statements about almost-sure localization.
